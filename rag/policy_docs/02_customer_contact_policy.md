# Customer Contact Policy

Customers may receive at most 1 recovery reminder (SMS/email/payment link)
per 24-hour window per transaction. A customer who has opted out of
recovery contact must never be messaged again for any transaction -
this is a hard stop with no exceptions.

Reminders should only be sent for USER_ABANDONMENT or BANK_DECLINE root
causes, where a passive nudge (rather than an automatic retry) is the
appropriate recovery action.
